# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People 45-70 with high blood pressure, mostly on medication, mostly women, reading at a
4th-grade level. They arrive warm from Joel's TikTok / Facebook / YouTube teaching or from
a podcast, on a phone, often anxious about their numbers and tired of being talked down to
by medical content. The job: understand what is driving THEIR blood pressure and get a
plain, doable plan they can run alongside their doctor.

## Product Purpose

BPQuiz.com is the storefront and funnel for The BP Triangle Method (Joel Polley, RN):
quiz → $17 kit → $27/mo Weekly Reset → $297 case review → 1:1. The BraveWorks BP iPhone
app (waitlist at /waitlist) extends the brand onto the phone: BP tracker, 10-Day Reset,
quiz, one-tap Doctor Report PDF, all on-device. Success = a visitor trusts Joel within one
viewport and takes the one action the page exists for.

## Brand Personality

Editorial apothecary. Clinical warmth. Quiet confidence. An ICU nurse of 20 years who
talks to you like a neighbor, not a hospital. Three words: **calm, credentialed, plain**.
Never hypey, never fear-mongering, never "wellness influencer."

## Anti-references

- Supplement-bro landing pages (countdown timers, red arrows, ALL CAPS claims)
- Sterile hospital-portal UI (cold blues, system fonts, form-first coldness)
- Generic AI SaaS gradients and glassmorphism
- Anything that promises outcomes ("lower your BP in 7 days") — regulatory + brand poison

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before ask.** Credential and warmth land before any form or price.
2. **4th-grade plain.** Short words, short sentences. If a nurse wouldn't say it out loud
   to a patient, cut it.
3. **AND not INSTEAD OF.** Every surface frames the work as alongside the doctor, never
   replacing meds or medical care. No health claims, ever. (NEWSTART Lens 2 is a ship gate.)
4. **One action per page.** Each page exists for exactly one next step.
5. **The paper apothecary is the identity.** Fraunces display + Inter body + JetBrains Mono
   numbers on the paper/ink/sage/clay palette (src/index.css tokens). Identity-preservation
   wins over trend rotation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Older audience on phones: WCAG AA contrast minimum, 16px+ body, generous touch targets,
visible labels on all inputs, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation. No
information carried by color alone.
