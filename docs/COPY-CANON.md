# COPY-CANON.md — the six shared moves

**Canonical voice for every BP-funnel page: `/` (CheckoutPage), `/challenge`
(ChallengePage), `/coaching` (BeThereLandingPage).**

Built 2026-08-04 from Omar Eltakori's Messiah Marketing Method, pattern
Matthew 11:28-30 (MSG). Three pages got the pass independently on the same day
and drifted into three vocabularies for the same ideas. This file is the
merge: **uniform meaning, different words per page.**

> Rule: a reader who lands on all three should feel one person wrote them, and
> should never read the same sentence twice. Port the MOVE, not the sentence.
> Where a phrase is marked HOUSE PHRASE, use it verbatim, because consistency
> there is worth more than variety.

---

## 1. Self-identify (question, not claim)

The passage does not ask "are you a sinner." It asks whether you are burned out
on the thing that was supposed to fix you. Every page opens by making the
on-medication reader raise her own hand.

- **Best on site (Challenge):** "Still taking the pill every morning, and still afraid of the cuff?"
- Homepage: "Tired of it? Not the pill. The life around the pill."
- Coaching: "Are You Tired of Being a Patient?"

All three are good and all three differ. Keep them different. The shared rule:
**name the failed remedy, not the diagnosis.**

## 2. The pain behind the pain

Not "your number is high." That is the surface. The wound is compliance without
reward, and what it cost her in self-trust.

- **Best on site (Challenge), port the idea everywhere:** the pill was supposed
  to be temporary, then it got a refill schedule and a permanent spot by the
  coffee maker, and what it quietly took with it was **the trust between you and
  your own body.**
- Coaching's version: "the only explanation left on the table was you." Then the
  turn: **"It was never you."**
- Homepage's version: "thirty years of managing instead of one honest season of addressing."

## 3. The forbidden promise, in its only safe form

She wants off the pills. We may never promise, imply, or hint at that. The
Challenge page solved this better than anywhere else, so it is now law.

> **HOUSE PHRASE:** *need less someday, earned honestly, with your doctor
> holding the pen.*

Supporting HOUSE PHRASE: *the conversation about needing less someday happens
with your doctor, never behind their back.*

**Never write:** get off your meds, reduce your dose, wean, taper, drug-free,
medication-free, "many clients no longer need." Not in copy, not in email, not
in a caption, not in a DM. A woman who stops a BP medication because our page
made her feel brave can stroke. The refusal is also the differentiator: saying
it plainly outsells the hint.

## 4. The vacation behind the vacation

One idea, three renderings, and the idea is **the end of surveillance.** Not
lower numbers. Not even the grandkids. Nobody watching her anymore, including
herself.

- Homepage: "a cuff that lives in a drawer."
- Challenge: "the people who love you stop watching you, and go back to just living with you."
- Coaching: "go a whole day without thinking about your blood pressure once, and only notice that night that you did not."

Any new page invents its own image of the same thing. Do not reuse another
page's image.

## 5. The ignorance tax

Reframes the decision: she is not choosing between spending and not spending.
She has been paying all along, in installments, with no education to show for
it.

- **Best definition (Homepage), port everywhere:** nobody ever hands you an
  invoice for it, you just keep paying. Another copay. Another refill. Another
  year of the same number and the same fifteen-minute appointment. **And the
  worry you carry between readings, that is part of the bill too.**
- **Best close (Coaching), port everywhere:** "You are not choosing between
  spending and not spending. You have been spending the whole time. You are
  choosing between a bill that never ends and one that does."

⚠️ **NO INVENTED DOLLAR TOTALS.** Name the categories, let her do the math. The
2026-07-25 audit found fabricated figures on this site once already, and
"you've spent $4,000 on supplements" is a number we do not have.

## 6. Dignity, at the close

She has been made to feel like the problem for years. One line undoes it.

> **HOUSE PHRASE (Challenge):** *You are not a difficult patient. You are an
> uninformed one, and that is fixable.*

This one goes on all three verbatim. It is the single most disarming sentence
in the funnel and it should sound familiar by the third time she meets it.

## Mechanics that apply to all three

- **Zero em dashes** in visible copy. Commas, periods, colons, parentheses.
- **Education alongside the doctor, never instead of it.** If we ever appear to
  contradict her doctor, her doctor wins. State it once per page, plainly, and
  do not apologize for it or repeat it every section.
- **No testimonials without a consent record** in `docs/testimonials.md`.
- **No invented value stacks, no phantom compare-at prices** (16 CFR 233).
- Follower and audience numbers come from `PLATFORM-STATS.md` only.

## When adding a fourth page

Work down the six moves in order. Write new words for moves 1, 2, 4 and 5. Use
the HOUSE PHRASES verbatim for move 3 and move 6. That is what makes it feel
uniform without reading like a template.
