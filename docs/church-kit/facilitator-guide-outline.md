# Facilitator Guide Outline: The BP Triangle for Your Church

**DRAFT for Joel's review. This is the outline the full guide will be written from.**

---

## Before Week 1: Facilitator basics (front section of the guide)

- **Who can lead:** any organized volunteer. You are a host and a guide, not a medical expert. The guide gives you every word you need.
- **Session shape (about 60 minutes):** welcome and opening prayer (5 min) → teach (20 min) → group activity (20 min) → home practice handout + scripture anchor (10 min) → closing prayer (5 min).
- **Three facilitator rules, said out loud in Week 1 and repeated every week:**
  1. We are not doctors. We never diagnose, treat, or tell anyone what to do with medication.
  2. Everything here works alongside your doctor, never instead of your doctor.
  3. What is shared in the group stays in the group.
- **Safety line (in the guide and on every workbook page):** if anyone reports a reading of 180/120 or higher, or has chest pain, trouble breathing, or sudden severe symptoms, urge them to seek medical care right away. Do not wait for next week's session.
- **Supplies:** printed workbook pages, the church's quiz link on a slide or handout, and (if the church has one) a blood pressure cuff for demonstration only.

---

## Week 1: The Triangle, and everyone takes the quiz

**20-minute teach:**
- Why we are here: nearly half of American adults have high blood pressure, and most were handed a pill but never a plan (5 min)
- The BP Triangle: Stress, Sugar, Sodium. Three everyday drivers you can actually work on (8 min)
- "Alongside your doctor, never instead of": what this program is and is not (4 min)
- How the 6 weeks work, what the workbook is for (3 min)

**Group activity:** everyone takes the 90-second quiz on their phone using the church's own link. Pairs share their loudest corner (the corner the quiz flagged strongest). No numbers required, only corners.

**Home practice:** start the daily BP log in the workbook (morning reading if you have a cuff; if not, note how you feel and when). Bring your log every week.

**Scripture anchor:** 1 Corinthians 6:19-20. Your body is a temple. Caring for it is stewardship, not vanity. We tend what God entrusted to us.

---

## Week 2: The Stress corner

**20-minute teach:**
- What stress does to blood pressure: the pressure system God built for emergencies, stuck in the "on" position (6 min)
- Paced breathing: breathe in slow through the nose for about 4 counts, out slower for about 6 counts, for 5 minutes. This is a physical skill, like slowing your walking pace. It is not meditation (6 min)
- Rest as a command, not a suggestion: sleep, real days off, and casting cares in prayer (5 min)
- Small stress levers: sunlight in the morning, a daily walk, less news before bed (3 min)

**Group activity:** the whole group practices paced breathing together for 5 minutes, led by the facilitator with the workbook card. Then pairs share one worry they can hand to God this week.

**Home practice:** paced breathing 5 minutes, twice a day, logged in the workbook. Keep the BP log going.

**Scripture anchor:** Philippians 4:6-7. Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer, present your requests to God. Prayer is the believer's first stress tool. The breathing card simply calms the body so the heart can pray.

---

## Week 3: The Sugar corner

**20-minute teach:**
- How sugar and refined carbs push blood pressure up over time (6 min)
- Where sugar hides: drinks, breakfast foods, sauces, "healthy" snacks (6 min)
- The plant-forward plate: whole grains, beans, vegetables, fruit, nuts. Simple swaps, not a diet overhaul (6 min)
- Water instead of sweet drinks: the easiest single win (2 min)

**Group activity:** "Find the sugar" label hunt. The facilitator brings 6 to 8 common food labels (or the workbook's printed versions). Teams rank them from least to most sugar, then check answers. The room is always surprised.

**Home practice:** swap one sweet drink for water every day this week, and try one plant-based supper from the workbook's simple recipe page. Log both.

**Scripture anchor:** Daniel 1:12. Daniel asked for vegetables and water for ten days, and stood the test. A simple food test, taken in faith, is an old and honorable idea.

---

## Week 4: The Sodium corner

**20-minute teach:**
- What salt does to blood pressure and why the body holds water to match it (5 min)
- The truth about the salt shaker: most sodium comes from packages and restaurants, not your shaker (6 min)
- Reading a sodium line on a label: what counts as high, what counts as low (5 min)
- Flavor without the flood: herbs, lemon, garlic, onion, and the workbook's seasoning swap chart (4 min)

**Group activity:** "Guess the sodium." Facilitator reads out common items (canned soup, bread, deli-style plant foods, frozen dinners) and the group guesses the sodium before the reveal. Highest wrong guess wins a friendly prize.

**Home practice:** check the sodium line on everything you eat for 3 days and write down the top 3 surprises. Try one seasoning swap from the chart.

**Scripture anchor:** Matthew 5:13. "You are the salt of the earth." Salt belongs in our witness more than in our supper. Let your life carry the flavor.

---

## Week 5: Doctor-partnership week: bring your numbers

**20-minute teach:**
- Why this program exists to make your doctor visits better, not to replace them (4 min)
- Your 4-week BP log is a gift to your doctor: what a pattern shows that one office reading cannot (6 min)
- The Doctor Visit Sheet: how to fill it in, what to ask, how to talk about the changes you have made (7 min)
- What never to do: never change or stop a medication on your own. Every change goes through your doctor (3 min)

**Group activity:** everyone fills in their Doctor Visit Sheet from their own log. Pairs practice saying their two questions out loud, like a rehearsal. The facilitator reminds the group: you are not asking permission to be healthy, you are inviting your doctor onto your team.

**Home practice:** book (or plan) a doctor visit and bring the sheet. If a visit is far off, mail or portal-message the log summary.

**Scripture anchor:** Proverbs 15:22. Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed. Scripture honors wise counsel, and Luke himself was a physician (Colossians 4:14). Doctors are part of God's provision.

---

## Week 6: Celebration, and what next

**20-minute teach:**
- Measure the gain, not the gap: look back at Week 1, not at a perfect ideal. What changed in 6 weeks? (6 min)
- The Triangle is a lifestyle, not a 6-week event: how to keep each corner alive (6 min)
- What next: retake the quiz on the church link and compare corners, keep the log going, and how the group can keep meeting monthly if it wants to (5 min)
- Thank-yous and testimony time setup (3 min)

**Group activity:** celebration meal (plant-based potluck, recipes in the workbook) and open testimony time. Members share what changed: a habit, a number, a doctor conversation, a burden lifted. Facilitator writes down (with permission) stories the ministry may share.

**Home practice:** retake the quiz this week and keep the daily log for 30 more days. Pick your one corner to keep working.

**Scripture anchor:** Philippians 1:6. He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion. Six weeks is a start, not a finish. God finishes what He starts, and so do His people.
