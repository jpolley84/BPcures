## What this is

Nearly half of American adults have high blood pressure. Many of them sit in your pews every Sunday.

The BP Triangle for Your Church is a 6-week, small-group program built for church health ministries. A leader from your church runs it. No medical training needed. Members meet once a week for about an hour. They learn the three everyday drivers of high blood pressure (Stress, Sugar, and Sodium) and simple, natural steps to work on each one, alongside their doctor.

It was built by Joel Polley, RN, a registered nurse with 20 years of ICU and ER experience. Joel teaches natural blood pressure care to a large online community and now brings the same teaching to churches, where health and faith already live side by side.

Research supports this model. A large study called the FAITH trial (published 2019) found that church-led lifestyle groups helped members lower their blood pressure more than health education alone. Your church is not just a nice place to do this. It may be the best place.

## Where we stand (please read this part)

- **Education, not medical care.** This program teaches. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. It never replaces anyone's doctor.
- **Alongside your doctor, never instead of.** Every week points members back to their own doctor. Week 5 is built entirely around preparing for a better doctor visit. No one is ever told to change or stop a medication.
- **Rooted in whole-person health.** The program follows time-tested natural health principles: whole plant foods, water, rest, sunshine, fresh air, self-control, and trust in God. It uses simple paced breathing for stress. No yoga, no meditation, no caffeine products, and no supplements to buy.
- **Honest by design.** No hype, no miracle claims, no pressure. Just a nurse teaching what he teaches his own community, in a room full of people who already care about each other.
