Girl.

Can I tell you something no one told me?

You sit down at the doctor.

They wrap the cuff. It tightens.

And the number is a little higher than last time.

And they say — kindly, quickly — *"Let's just keep an eye on it. Maybe cut the salt. Lose a few pounds."*

And you nod.

You say thank you.

You walk out to the car.

And then you just… sit there for a minute.

Because it doesn't feel like "just watch it."

It feels like something's shifting and nobody's really explaining it.

Here's what I need you to hear today, nurse to friend:

**you are not imagining it.**

And it is not because you failed at salt.

Here's the part almost nobody connects for a woman your age:

your body is going through *the change* —

and the change doesn't stop at hot flashes and sleep.

It reaches all the way to your heart.

To your numbers.

As things shift in menopause, blood pressure and heart risk quietly rise for so many women —

and most of us were never told the two were even related.

Nobody sat you down and said: *"By the way — the thing happening to your cycle? It's connected to the thing happening on that cuff."*

So you've been carrying two separate worries…

that were secretly the same one.

That's not you being dramatic.

That's biology no one bothered to draw for you.

You were fearfully, wonderfully made.

Your body isn't betraying you.

It's *talking* to you.

And this week, I'm going to help you understand exactly what it's saying.

Tomorrow — well, in two days — I'll show you *where* this actually lives in the body.

Not a lecture. I promise.

The thing that finally made it make sense for me.

**One thing before then.**

You know a woman who needs to read this.

Your sister. Your mom. The friend who went quiet in her own car last week.

Forward it to her.

She's been carrying it alone too.

I'm so glad you're here.

Annie
