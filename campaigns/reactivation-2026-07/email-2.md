Last time I told you about the BP Triangle. Stress, sugar, sodium. Three corners, and one of them is usually loudest.

Today, the corner most people skip: **stress**.

Here is what happens in your body. When you are worried, your body pumps out stress hormones. Those hormones tell your blood vessels to squeeze tight. Tighter pipes mean higher pressure. It is that simple, and it is that sneaky.

Your pill does not know you are worried. It cannot touch that part. Only you can.

**One thing to try tonight.** It is free and takes 10 minutes. It is called paced breathing.

1. Take your blood pressure and write it down.
2. Sit somewhere quiet. Feet flat on the floor. Screens off.
3. Breathe in slow through your nose for a count of 4.
4. Breathe out even slower for a count of 6.
5. Keep going for 10 minutes. In for 4, out for 6.
6. Take your pressure again. Write both numbers down.

Slow, paced breathing tells your body the emergency is over. For many people, the second number is lower than the first. I cannot promise what yours will do. But it costs nothing, and nobody ever got hurt by breathing slow.

Me? I like to pray while I do it. A slow breath in, a short prayer on the way out. That is just how I was raised, and it works for me.

Try it every night this week. Watch what your evening readings do over seven days, not one.
