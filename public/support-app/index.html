Common questions

How do I log a reading?
Tap Log Reading on the Home screen or the + button on the Tracker tab. Only fill in what you have — every field is optional except your BP numbers.

How do I make the Doctor Report PDF?
Tap Doctor Report, choose a date range (7 / 30 / 90 days / All), and tap Generate. You can email it, print it, or save it — one page, ready to hand to your doctor.

How do I set the daily reminder?
Settings → Daily Reminder. Turn it on and pick a time. It's a local notification on your phone — nothing is sent to any server.

How do I switch between lb and kg?
Settings → Weight Units. This changes the label only; your saved numbers stay as you entered them.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Clear My Data erases everything permanently. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Where does my data go?
Nowhere. Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud. See the Privacy Policy.
