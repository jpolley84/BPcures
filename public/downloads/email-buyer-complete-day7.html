Hi Sarah,

A week in. By now you have felt how one corner calms when you stop feeding it. Here is the thing to hold as you move through your Triangle: it is a loop, and the corners feed each other.

Even a perfectly calmed stress corner gets tugged back up if sugar is still spiking insulin, because insulin tells your kidneys to hold sodium. That is why you walk all three. When you have closed every side, the Freedom Finale teaches you the part almost no one is ever taught: how to step back from daily policing and trust the body you rebuilt, with your doctor right beside you the whole way.

 
Your rhythm
Finish your current corner, then move to the next one in the module. Keep one keystone habit from each corner as you go (your sleep and breath, your steady blood sugar, your own cooking and water). Those keystones are what hold the gains for life.

Your full kit is always here: your library. It does not expire. Return to any corner for a week as a tune up whenever the loop starts heating up again.
