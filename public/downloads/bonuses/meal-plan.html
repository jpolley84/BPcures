The BraveWorks Triangle Meal Plan

Big breakfast. Lighter as the day goes. Plant-based, high-fiber, mostly raw. Built to feed all three corners, Stress, Sugar, and Sodium, at once.

The pattern (this is the whole secret)

Front-load your food. Eat your biggest meal at breakfast, a medium lunch, and a light supper, or no supper at all. You eat with the sun and rest your body at night, the way it was designed to run. Between meals, water only. No snacking, no grazing. Giving your stomach four to five hours of rest between meals is half of why this works.

Why it works for your numbers:
  • Front-loading food early supports a healthy weight and steadier blood sugar, the Sugar corner.
  • High fiber, especially raw and from beans and oats, slows sugar, fills you up, and helps your body let go of what it does not need.
  • Whole plant foods are naturally rich in potassium and low in sodium, which is exactly the balance the Sodium corner needs.
  • Eating calmly, earlier, and lighter at night supports rest and lowers the evening stress load, the Stress corner.

Start every meal with something raw. Raw fruit or a raw salad first primes digestion. Then the cooked portion.

Your 7-Day Plan (repeat it each week)

Repeat the week as long as you like. Swap days around to fit your schedule, just keep the shape: big breakfast, medium lunch, light or no supper.

Day 1
Big breakfast

A bowl of fresh fruit (berries, banana, orange), then steel-cut oats with ground flaxseed, chia, walnuts, and cinnamon.

Medium lunch

A large raw salad (leafy greens, tomato, cucumber, carrot, beet, red cabbage, lemon and olive oil), then lentils over brown rice.

Light supper or skip

A fresh apple and a handful of berries.

Day 2
Big breakfast

Sliced melon and grapes, then sprouted whole-grain toast with mashed avocado, tomato, and lemon.

Medium lunch

A raw cabbage and apple slaw, then a baked sweet potato with black beans, salsa, and avocado.

Light supper or skip

Raw vegetable sticks (celery, carrot, cucumber, pepper) with hummus.

Day 3
Big breakfast

A fresh fruit bowl, then overnight oats with chia, ground flax, blueberries, and chopped almonds.

Medium lunch

A big green salad, then a bowl of white-bean and vegetable soup with a slice of whole-grain sourdough.

Light supper or skip

A green smoothie (spinach, banana, frozen berries, ground flax).

Day 4
Big breakfast

Sliced banana and berries, then a tofu scramble with turmeric, spinach, onion, and peppers, plus whole-grain toast.

Medium lunch

A raw salad first, then a quinoa bowl with chickpeas, roasted vegetables, cucumber, and lemon-tahini.

Light supper or skip

A fresh pear and an orange.

Day 5
Big breakfast

Orange and kiwi, then millet or buckwheat porridge with dates, pumpkin seeds, and cinnamon, topped with fresh fruit.

Medium lunch

A large mixed-greens salad, then a lentil and vegetable stew over brown rice.

Light supper or skip

Raw carrot and cucumber with guacamole.

Day 6
Big breakfast

A big fruit bowl, then whole-grain porridge with walnuts, ground flax, banana, and cinnamon.

Medium lunch

A raw cabbage and carrot slaw, then a baked potato with steamed broccoli, chickpeas, and a lemon-tahini drizzle.

Light supper or skip

A handful of grapes and an apple.

Day 7
Big breakfast

Sliced papaya or melon and berries, then oatmeal with chia, ground flax, almonds, and cinnamon.

Medium lunch

A large raw salad, then black-bean and vegetable chili over brown rice or quinoa.

Light supper or skip

A green smoothie or fresh fruit.

Between meals, water only, six to eight glasses a day, mostly between meals rather than during them. Herbal teas are fine. No coffee, no energy drinks.

For maximum weight loss and blood pressure control: the two-meal day

When you are ready, drop supper entirely. Eat a hearty breakfast and a medium lunch, finish your last bite by early afternoon, and take only water or herbal tea until breakfast the next morning. This long nightly rest is one of the most powerful, and most overlooked, levers for both weight and blood pressure. Build up to it gradually. Do not force it on day one.

Important, please read

If you take medication for blood pressure or blood sugar, talk to your doctor before you cut to two meals or skip supper. As your eating changes, your numbers can fall, and your doctor may need to adjust your medication so they do not fall too far. This plan is education to bring to your doctor and to use alongside their care, never instead of it.

The short version

  • Big breakfast, medium lunch, light or no supper.
  • Plant foods only, whole and unprocessed. Lots of raw.
  • High fiber every meal: fruit, vegetables, beans, oats, nuts, seeds.
  • No added salt, no refined sugar, no snacking, no caffeine.
  • Water between meals.
  • Finish eating early.
