Privacy Policy

BraveWorks BP (iOS app) · Effective July 10, 2026

The short version: BraveWorks BP does not collect, transmit, or share your health data. Your blood pressure readings, heart rate, weight, and mood entries are stored only on your device. No analytics, no accounts, no cloud.

Who we are

BraveWorks BP is published by BraveWorks RN (Joel Polley, RN). Questions about this policy can be sent to support@bpquiz.com.

What the app stores — and where

Everything you enter in BraveWorks BP lives only in the app's local storage on your phone:

  • Blood pressure readings (systolic, diastolic), heart rate, weight, mood, and notes
  • Your first name and display preferences (like your weight unit and reminder time), if you choose to set them
  • Your quiz result and challenge progress

None of this ever leaves your device. The app has no server, no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

What we do NOT do

  • No collection or transmission of health data
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or trackers
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No selling, sharing, or renting of any data — there is nothing to sell

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reading reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled entirely by iOS on your phone. No push-notification service or server is involved, and no token is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

You are in full control. Open Settings → Clear My Data inside the app to permanently erase all readings, quiz results, challenge progress, and preferences. Deleting the app from your phone also removes all of its data.

Children

BraveWorks BP is intended for adults. The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud-sync feature), this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that version ships.

Contact

Email support@bpquiz.com with any privacy questions.

BraveWorks BP is for educational tracking only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for professional care. Always consult your physician. Never start, stop, or change any medication without your doctor.
