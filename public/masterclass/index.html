Take Charge of Your Numbers

Insights from a registered nurse with 20 years at the bedside.

I get it.

For twenty years I stood at the bedside in the ICU and the ER. I remember one man in particular — sixty-four, three blood pressure medications, taking every pill exactly on schedule. He kept the list in his wallet on a folded index card, soft at the creases. And the number on the monitor above his head still would not come down.

They added a fourth pill. Then a fifth. A few months later he was back — same numbers, one more line on the index card in someone else's handwriting.

Standing at that bed at two in the morning, I kept asking the same question: why is nobody telling him what's actually driving this?

Because the numbers on that cuff aren't random. Three hidden daily triggers are behind them — and almost nobody connects the dots.

So I built this class. For the people I couldn't reach from a hospital room. For the mom, the grandma, the man with a purpose still to live out. And for you.
