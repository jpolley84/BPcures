CAN I ASK YOU SOMETHING?

When was the last time someone took a picture of you, and you actually LOVED it?

Not after finding the perfect angle. Not after putting a filter on it. Not after taking 15 pictures and deleting 14.

Just you. Smiling. Looking like a woman who's happy to be in the picture.

Girl, I know.

Sometimes you look at an old photograph and think... there she is.

Maybe her hair was fuller. Maybe she had more energy. Maybe she loved getting dressed up.

Or maybe it was how she FELT. She laughed more. She went places. She didn't spend so much time worrying about what people thought about her. She had a certain spark.

And somewhere between raising children, working, building a life, caring for everybody else, and going through all these changes in her body... she stopped feeling like THAT girl.

You do not have to be 25 again to feel beautiful at 55, 65, or 75.

You don't need somebody else's face. You don't need somebody else's body. You just want to feel good in YOURS.

You are not wrong for wanting your skin to have life in it again, your hair to bounce, and your eyes to sparkle. Getting your glow back is about more than what you put on your skin.
