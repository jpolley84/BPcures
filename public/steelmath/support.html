How accurate is the math?

Dimensions are stored as whole sixty-fourths of an inch, so fractions add exactly with no rounding drift. Rafter per-foot multipliers match the table on a framing square: check a 6/12 common against yours and you should read 13.4164 inches per foot of run, hip/valley exactly 18.
