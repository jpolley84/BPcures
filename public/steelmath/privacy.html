SteelMath — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-14

SteelMath collects no data. None.

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own. Every calculation happens on your device and nothing you type is transmitted or stored anywhere but your phone.

The one-time SteelMath Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple ID, or your payment details. The app stores a single local flag recording that the purchase was made, so the tools keep working with no signal.

Deleting the app removes that flag along with everything else. Reinstalling and tapping Restore Purchases brings your purchase back at no charge.
