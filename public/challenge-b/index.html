Frequently Asked Questions.

What if I miss a session?

Each core session is one hour. Replays are available for 48 hours, so if life happens, you can catch up while the challenge is still moving.

What if I'm already on medication or working with my doctor?

Come. This challenge does not replace your healthcare provider, and Annie and Joel will not tell you to stop or change prescribed medication. The goal is to help you understand your own health information, routines and patterns so you can make better-informed decisions with your care team.

What if my labs say I'm “normal,” but I still don't feel normal?

Come. A lab result is one piece of information. So are the changes you've noticed in your energy, sleep, weight, mood, hair, blood pressure, blood sugar and how you feel day to day. We'll help you look at the bigger pattern instead of assuming “normal” means there is nothing worth paying attention to.

What if I'm older - or I don't have every symptom on this page?

You do not need every symptom, and you are not too old. Whether it's one thing getting your attention or five things happening at once, if your body has changed and you know something deserves your attention, start there.

Do I need to buy supplements, special foods or equipment?

No. Your $97 and the things you already have are enough to complete the challenge.

Do I have to be on camera or share private health information?

No. Participate as much as you're comfortable. You never have to share anything you want to keep private.

Where does the challenge happen?

Live on Zoom at 7 PM Eastern / 6 PM Central. We'll send you the link and simple instructions. You can join from a phone, tablet or computer.

Is $97 the full price, and is there anything else to buy?
