What women ask us

What if I miss a day?

Replays are up for 48 hours after each live session, so a busy Tuesday won't knock you out of the challenge. But whenever you can, show up live. The power of this week is in the doing, not the watching.

What if I'm on medication?

Come. This challenge does not replace your healthcare provider, and we will never tell you to stop or change a prescribed medication. You'll learn to observe your own routines, habits, and health information more carefully. That means better conversations with your healthcare team, not skipping them.

What if I'm older than 40?

Good. This isn't about turning back the clock. You still have places to go, people to love, grandchildren to enjoy, trips to take, things to build, and wisdom the world still needs from you. Your health should help you be there for it.

What if I've tried everything?

Then stop trying everything. Start paying attention. Read it. Reset it. Reclaim it. Three days. Let's find your next move.

Why are seats actually limited?

Two reasons, both real. The Zoom room only holds so many women if we're going to coach you live instead of just talking at a camera. And we don't have a confirmed date for the next Change My Life Challenge yet. If you wait, you may be waiting a while.

What if I do the challenge and it isn't for me?

Joining the waitlist costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You are telling us to hold you a spot and send you the details first. When the dates and the price land in your inbox, you decide then.

This is not medical advice or a substitute for care from your own provider.
