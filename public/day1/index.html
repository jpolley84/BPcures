Part 3 · About 4 minutes

Look for the Pattern

Now we're going to take everything you just wrote and look through the Blood Pressure Triangle™.

Do not try to diagnose yourself. You are looking for clues.

Before you take the quiz, answer from your gut.

Stress

Have I been living in a season of constant pressure, worry, caregiving, rushing, poor sleep, or never really turning “off”? Do I notice my readings or symptoms acting differently during stressful seasons?

Sugar

Have my blood sugar, A1C, belly weight, hunger, cravings, or energy changed together? Do I notice crashes, strong cravings, or big changes in how I feel around meals?

Sodium

How much of what I eat comes from restaurants, packaged foods, prepared foods, or foods where I don't really know the sodium? Do I notice certain meals or eating patterns showing up around changes in my readings?

Hormone changes

Did some of these changes become louder as my body changed in my 40s, 50s, 60s, or beyond? Did things like sleep, belly weight, energy, mood, hot flashes, hair, or other body changes begin showing up during the same season?
