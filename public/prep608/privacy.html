608 Prep — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-13

608 Prep collects no data. None.

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests. Your study progress, quiz scores, and missed-question history are stored only in a local database on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The optional daily study reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

608 Prep is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. EPA or any certifying organization.
