608 Prep — Support

608 Prep is a study app for the EPA Section 608 technician certification exam: 225 practice questions with explanations, mock exams in the real 25-question format, and missed-question tracking. Fully offline.

Common questions

Is my progress backed up? Progress lives on your device only (that's the privacy promise). Deleting the app resets progress.

Is this the official exam? No — it's an independent study tool based on the public federal regulations (40 CFR Part 82). You take the real exam through an EPA-approved certifying organization.

Found a question you think is wrong? Email us the question text — content accuracy reports get priority.
