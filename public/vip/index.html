What if I'm already on blood pressure medication?

You're welcome. Blood pressure medications are important, and you should continue taking yours as prescribed. This challenge helps you understand patterns and everyday changes you can discuss with your clinician. We do not take people off their medication.

What if I've tried every diet, tea and natural remedy?

That's exactly why this is about finding your Big Domino, not buying another bottle or collecting another list of fixes. We'll help you connect the dots and decide what deserves your attention first.

What if I don't have much time?

Give yourself five focused days. You'll have live training, a clear theme for each day and 48-hour replay access if you miss a session. You don't have to overhaul your life this week.

Is this for successful business owners or women enjoying retirement?

Both. Whether you run a business, care for family, lead at work, serve your community or want more freedom in retirement, the point is the same: you need your health to support the life you're building.

What if I'm afraid it will be another program I don't finish?

This is live and focused, with a community and two nurses guiding you through the experience. You'll have one clear question to answer: “What deserves MY attention first?” Your participation matters more than perfection.

What does VIP add?

Everything in the five-day live challenge (community, group Q&A, daily guides and replays), plus extended VIP coaching and Q&A access, priority question submission, a bonus session on your numbers and next steps, and the VIP workbook. VIP is regularly $197. Tonight it is $97.

Can I stop my medication if my numbers improve?

No one should stop or change blood pressure medication based on a challenge or a single reading. Work with your prescribing clinician to review any changes; abruptly stopping some blood pressure medicines can be dangerous.
